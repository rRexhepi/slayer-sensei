Data in this plugin

The task notes (slayer_tasks.json and slayer_tasks_supplement.json) and the places data (slayer_areas.json)
were written from pages of the Old School RuneScape Wiki, https://oldschool.runescape.wiki
Wiki content is licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/
The wiki's licensing terms are at https://meta.weirdgloop.org/w/Licensing
The source fields in slayer_areas.json name the wiki page each point and fact came from.

Entrance points also come from the world map data of the RuneLite client, https://github.com/runelite/runelite
RuneLite is under the BSD 2-Clause License.
